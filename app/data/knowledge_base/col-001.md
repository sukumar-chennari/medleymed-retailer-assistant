# Cetirizine 10mg Antihistamine Tablets

## Overview
Cetirizine is a second-generation antihistamine that relieves runny nose,
sneezing, and watery/itchy eyes — cold symptoms driven by histamine release,
as well as allergy symptoms. It's generally less sedating than older
first-generation antihistamines.

## Dosage
Adults typically take 1 tablet (10mg) once daily. It can be taken with or
without food. Do not exceed one dose in 24 hours.

## Common Side Effects
The most commonly reported side effect is mild drowsiness, though it's less
sedating than older antihistamines. Dry mouth and mild headache have also
been reported.

## Warnings
Use caution when driving or operating machinery until you know how it affects
you, since drowsiness can occur. Avoid alcohol, which can increase drowsiness.
People with kidney problems may need a lower dose — consult a doctor or
pharmacist first.
