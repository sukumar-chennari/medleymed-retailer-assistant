# Children's Cold & Cough Syrup

## Overview
A liquid combination product formulated for children, pairing a mild dry-cough
suppressant with an antihistamine to relieve a dry cough alongside runny nose
and sneezing during a cold. Designed to be easier and safer to dose accurately
for kids than an adult combination product.

## Dosage
Dosage is based on weight and age, not a flat adult dose — always use the
dosing cup or syringe provided and follow the weight/age chart on the label
exactly. Not intended for children under the minimum age listed on the label
(commonly 4 or 6 years, depending on the product) — check with a doctor or
pharmacist for younger children.

## Common Side Effects
Generally well tolerated when dosed correctly by weight. Mild drowsiness is
the most commonly reported side effect, from the antihistamine component.
Rare side effects include stomach upset.

## Warnings
Many health authorities recommend against cough and cold medicines for very
young children — always check the minimum age on the label and consult a
doctor or pharmacist before use, especially for children under 6. Not
intended for a wet, productive cough — this is a dry-cough suppressant
combination. Do not use alongside any other product containing an
antihistamine or cough suppressant. Consult a doctor if symptoms persist
more than a few days or are accompanied by a high fever.
