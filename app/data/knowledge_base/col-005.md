# Guaifenesin Expectorant Syrup

## Overview
Guaifenesin is an expectorant that thins and loosens mucus in the airways,
making a wet, productive cough more effective at clearing congestion —
unlike a cough suppressant, it doesn't stop the cough, it helps the cough
do its job.

## Dosage
Adults typically take the dose listed on the label (commonly 200-400mg)
every 4 hours as needed, not exceeding 6 doses (2400mg) in 24 hours. Drink
plenty of water alongside it, since fluids help loosen mucus too.

## Common Side Effects
Generally well tolerated. Mild nausea, upset stomach, or headache have been
reported, usually at higher doses.

## Warnings
Not intended for a persistent cough caused by smoking, asthma, or emphysema,
or a cough accompanied by excessive phlegm, unless directed by a doctor. See
a doctor if the cough lasts more than 7 days, comes back, or is accompanied
by fever, rash, or a persistent headache.
