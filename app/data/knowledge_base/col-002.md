# Pseudoephedrine Decongestant Tablets

## Overview
Pseudoephedrine is a decongestant that relieves nasal and sinus congestion by
narrowing swollen blood vessels in the nasal passages, making it easier to
breathe during a cold or sinus infection.

## Dosage
Adults typically take 1 tablet (60mg) every 4-6 hours as needed, not exceeding
4 doses in 24 hours. Because it can be stimulating, avoid taking a dose too
close to bedtime.

## Common Side Effects
Common side effects include nervousness, dizziness, trouble sleeping, or a
mild increase in heart rate, since it works by stimulating blood vessels to
constrict.

## Warnings
Not recommended for people with high blood pressure, heart disease, or
thyroid conditions without first consulting a doctor, since it can raise
blood pressure and heart rate. Avoid combining with other decongestants or
stimulants. Many pharmacies require ID to purchase this product due to
regulations on pseudoephedrine sales.
