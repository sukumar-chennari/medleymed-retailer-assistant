# Ibuprofen 200mg Tablets

## Overview
Ibuprofen is a nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drug (NSAID) that reduces fever,
relieves pain, and reduces inflammation — making it a good option for fevers
that come with body aches, headaches, or muscle pain, in addition to the fever
itself.

## Dosage
Adults typically take 1-2 tablets (200-400mg) every 4-6 hours as needed, not
exceeding 6 tablets (1200mg) in 24 hours for over-the-counter use, unless
directed otherwise by a doctor. Taking it with food or milk can help reduce
stomach upset.

## Common Side Effects
Common side effects include stomach upset, heartburn, or mild nausea. Taking
ibuprofen with food can reduce the likelihood of stomach irritation.

## Warnings
Not recommended for people with a history of stomach ulcers, kidney problems,
or certain heart conditions — consult a doctor or pharmacist first if any of
these apply. Avoid combining with other NSAIDs. People who are pregnant,
especially in the third trimester, should avoid ibuprofen unless a doctor
advises otherwise.
