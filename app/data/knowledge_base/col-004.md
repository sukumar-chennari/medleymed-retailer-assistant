# Cough Suppressant Syrup (Dextromethorphan)

## Overview
Dextromethorphan is a cough suppressant that works on the brain's cough
reflex to reduce dry, persistent coughing — the kind of cough that isn't
producing mucus and mainly serves to keep you (and everyone around you)
awake at night.

## Dosage
Adults typically take the dose listed on the label (commonly 10-20mg) every
4 hours as needed, not exceeding the maximum daily dose on the label. Use the
measuring cup provided rather than a regular spoon.

## Common Side Effects
Generally well tolerated at labeled doses. Mild dizziness, drowsiness, or
stomach upset have been reported.

## Warnings
Not intended for a "wet" or productive cough (one that's bringing up mucus),
since suppressing that kind of cough isn't usually recommended — check with a
pharmacist if you're unsure which type of cough you have. Do not combine with
other cough/cold products that also contain dextromethorphan. Should not be
combined with certain antidepressant medications (MAOIs) — consult a doctor
or pharmacist if you take other prescription medication.
