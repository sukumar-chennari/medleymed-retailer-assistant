# Paracetamol 500mg Tablets

## Overview
Paracetamol (also known as acetaminophen) is a widely used over-the-counter
pain reliever and fever reducer. It's generally considered gentler on the
stomach than NSAIDs like ibuprofen, which is why it's often recommended as a
first choice for reducing fever and relieving mild to moderate pain.

## Dosage
Adults typically take 1-2 tablets (500-1000mg) every 4-6 hours as needed, not
exceeding 8 tablets (4000mg) in 24 hours. Always follow the dosing instructions
on the product label, and do not take more than the labeled maximum.

## Common Side Effects
When taken as directed, paracetamol is generally well tolerated. Rare side
effects can include nausea, stomach discomfort, or allergic skin reactions.

## Warnings
Do not combine with other products that also contain paracetamol/acetaminophen,
as this can lead to accidental overdose. People with liver problems, or who
drink alcohol regularly, should consult a doctor or pharmacist before use.
Seek medical attention immediately if you suspect an overdose, even if you feel
fine, since liver damage from paracetamol overdose can be delayed.
