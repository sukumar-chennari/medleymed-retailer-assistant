# Paracetamol Extra Strength 650mg

## Overview
A higher-dose formulation of paracetamol (acetaminophen), intended for adults
who need stronger fever reduction or pain relief than the standard 500mg
tablet provides, without switching to a different active ingredient.

## Dosage
Adults typically take 1 tablet (650mg) every 6-8 hours as needed, not
exceeding 4000mg of total paracetamol in 24 hours from all sources combined.
Because this is a higher per-tablet dose, it's especially important not to
also take other paracetamol-containing products at the same time.

## Common Side Effects
Similar to standard-strength paracetamol: generally well tolerated, with rare
side effects including nausea, stomach discomfort, or allergic skin reactions.

## Warnings
Do not combine with any other product containing paracetamol/acetaminophen,
including combination cold/flu products — check labels carefully, since
accidental double-dosing is the most common risk with extra-strength
formulations. People with liver problems, or who drink alcohol regularly,
should consult a doctor or pharmacist before use.
