# Children's Paracetamol Syrup

## Overview
A liquid formulation of paracetamol designed for children, making it easier
to dose accurately by body weight and easier for kids to take than a tablet.
Used to reduce fever and relieve mild pain in children.

## Dosage
Dosage for children is based on weight and age, not a flat adult dose — always
use the dosing cup or syringe provided with the product and follow the
weight/age chart on the label exactly. Do not estimate the dose using a
regular kitchen spoon, as this can lead to inaccurate dosing.

## Common Side Effects
Generally well tolerated when dosed correctly by weight. Rare side effects can
include mild stomach upset or allergic skin reactions.

## Warnings
Children's liver and kidney systems process medication differently than
adults, so accurate weight-based dosing matters more here than with adult
products. Do not give alongside any other product containing paracetamol.
Consult a doctor before use in infants under the age listed on the label, or
if fever persists more than a couple of days.
