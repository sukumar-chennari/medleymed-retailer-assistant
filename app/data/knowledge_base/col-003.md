# Cold & Flu Multi-Symptom Relief

## Overview
A combination product containing paracetamol, phenylephrine, and
chlorpheniramine — covering fever/aches, nasal congestion, and runny
nose/sneezing in one product, for people who'd otherwise need to take three
separate medicines during a cold or flu.

## Dosage
Adults typically take 1-2 tablets every 4-6 hours as needed, not exceeding
the maximum daily dose listed on the label. Because this product already
contains paracetamol, do not take any other paracetamol-containing product
alongside it.

## Common Side Effects
May cause drowsiness (from the chlorpheniramine component), mild nervousness
or dizziness (from the phenylephrine component), or dry mouth. Combination
products can have more side effects than single-ingredient options since
they combine three active ingredients.

## Warnings
Check the active ingredients against anything else you're taking — this is
a combination product, so double-dosing on paracetamol or decongestants is
the main risk if taken alongside other cold/flu remedies. Not recommended for
people with high blood pressure or heart conditions without consulting a
doctor first, due to the phenylephrine component. May cause drowsiness — use
caution when driving.
